# Community expectations

Help people learn, including people using coding tools for the first time. Discuss behaviour and evidence clearly, assume good intent, and welcome different levels of experience.

Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, publish another person's private information, or pressure contributors to share credentials. Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project. Critique technical choices without personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. For a concern, contact the maintainer through a contact method they publish on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/AtharvaBondre), or use GitHub's platform reporting controls. Do not post private incident details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
